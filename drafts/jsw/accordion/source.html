Frequently asked questions

How do I reserve a JSW Motors vehicle?

Fill in the reservation form with your details and city, then pay the refundable reservation amount online. You will receive a confirmation by email straight away.

Go to the reservation form

Is the reservation amount refundable?

Yes. If you change your mind before your vehicle is allocated, the full amount is refunded to your original payment method within seven working days.

Where can I charge my car?

Charge at home overnight with the wall charger, or top up on the go at public fast chargers across the country. The companion app shows the chargers near you.

What does the warranty cover?

Every vehicle comes with a comprehensive warranty on the vehicle and a separate, longer warranty on the battery pack and the electric drive.

How do I book a service?

Book a service in the companion app or call your nearest service centre. Many checks can be done at your home or office.
